Celebrations and Festivals of Zamboanga City

Zamboanga City is known for its colorful festivals, religious celebrations, traditional music, dances, and cultural activities. These celebrations reflect the city's diverse heritage and help preserve the traditions and identity of the Zamboangueño people.

Fiesta Pilar • October

Zamboanga Hermosa Festival

Fiesta Hermosa, also known as the Zamboanga Hermosa Festival or Fiesta Pilar, is one of Zamboanga City's biggest annual celebrations. It is held every October in honor of Nuestra Señora La Virgen del Pilar (Our Lady of the Pillar), the city's patroness. The main feast day is October 12.

The celebration includes religious activities, street dancing, cultural presentations, musical performances, food festivals, and community gatherings. It brings together people from different cultural and religious communities to celebrate Zamboanga's heritage and traditions.

The festival is closely connected to Fort Pilar, an important historical and religious landmark.

Why it is celebrated To honor Our Lady of the Pillar and showcase the city's faith, cultural diversity, and traditions.

Civic life and language

City and Community Celebrations

Public observances recognize Zamboanga's civic history and the language and culture that shape local identity.

February 26

Dia de la Ciudad de Zamboanga

Dia de la Ciudad de Zamboanga is celebrated every February 26 to commemorate the city's inauguration as a chartered city in 1937. This occasion marks an important milestone in Zamboanga's history and recognizes its development as an independent city with its own local government.

The celebration is also an opportunity for residents to reflect on the city's progress, honor the contributions of its people, and express pride in their shared identity. Through commemorative activities and civic events, the occasion highlights Zamboanga's history, achievements, and continuing development.

Why it matters Dia de la Ciudad reminds residents of the city's historical beginnings as a chartered city and encourages appreciation for its heritage, progress, and community.
June 23

Dia de Fundacion de Chavacano (Chavacano Foundation Day)

Celebrated every June 23, Dia de Fundacion de Chavacano recognizes and helps preserve the Chavacano language, an important part of Zamboanga's cultural identity. The date is connected to June 23, 1635, when the cornerstone of Fort Pilar, originally known as Fuerza de San Jose, was laid.

According to the city's Ordinance No. 374, this historical period is associated with the development of Chavacano through interactions among workers from different parts of the Philippines, local communities, and Spanish authorities.

Language-related activities, cultural programs, and educational competitions encourage younger generations to appreciate and preserve Chavacano.

Why it is celebrated To promote the use, appreciation, and preservation of Chavacano as part of Zamboanga's language and cultural heritage.

Fiesta Hermosa • Maritime heritage

Regatta de Zamboanga

Regatta de Zamboanga is a traditional boat-racing competition and one of the major highlights of the Fiesta Hermosa Festival. It features colorful traditional vintas sailing across the waters of Zamboanga.

The boats display beautiful sails in vibrant colors and geometric patterns, representing the maritime heritage of Sama, Badjao, and other coastal communities. The event celebrates the city's connection to the sea, traditional boat craftsmanship, and the seafaring lifestyle of its people.

Regatta de Zamboanga is also an attraction for local and international visitors.

Why it is celebrated To preserve Zamboanga's maritime traditions and showcase the beauty of its colorful vintas and coastal culture.

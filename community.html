People • Identity • Belonging

Our Community, Our Identity

Get to know the people, cultural identities, and shared experiences that contribute to the diverse character of Zamboanga City.

Understanding our community

A city shaped by different people and cultures

A community is made up of people who share places, experiences, relationships, and responsibilities. Zamboanga City is home to different cultural and religious communities. Their languages, histories, livelihoods, and cultural expressions contribute to the city's identity.

One city, many identities

Understanding Zamboanga means recognizing that its residents do not all have the same background or cultural practices. Each community has its own history and identity, and these differences deserve recognition and respect.

Cultural communities

Different identities, shared spaces

Language and identity

Chavacano-speaking Community

Chavacano is an important part of Zamboanga City's linguistic and cultural identity. It is used in everyday communication and is connected to the city's history and experiences.

Promoting its use through conversation, literature, education, and creative expression helps support its continued transmission to younger generations.

Indigenous artistry

Yakan and Other Bangsamoro Communities

Yakan communities are recognized for their weaving traditions and cultural practices. Traditional textiles are expressions of craftsmanship, knowledge, and cultural identity.

The wider Zamboanga Peninsula is also home to other Bangsamoro communities whose cultural practices and histories contribute to the region's heritage.

Community life

Families and Local Communities

Families, neighborhoods, schools, and local organizations are important parts of community life. They provide spaces where people build relationships, share experiences, and support one another.

Cooperation and respect

Living Together

People from different backgrounds share public spaces, workplaces, schools, and community activities. Understanding differences and communicating respectfully can help strengthen relationships.

Community values

Building respect and understanding

Cultural understanding begins with recognizing that people have different experiences, beliefs, and ways of life. Respectful interaction helps create a community where people can learn from one another without judging or stereotyping others.

1. Respect cultural differences

Recognize that cultural practices may differ between communities. Avoid assuming that one group's customs represent everyone.

2. Listen and learn

Listen to people when they share their experiences. Learn about cultural practices from reliable sources and, when appropriate, from community members themselves.

3. Respect cultural expressions

Traditional clothing, textiles, designs, music, and other cultural expressions may carry specific meanings. Understand their significance instead of treating them only as decorations.

4. Support cultural preservation

Support responsible cultural education, local artisans, community programs, and efforts to preserve languages and traditional knowledge.

5. Practice consideration

Ask permission before taking photographs of people or private cultural activities. Follow the guidance of community members when visiting cultural spaces.

Our shared responsibility

Preserving cultural identity is not only about remembering the past. It also involves respecting the people who continue to practice, teach, and protect their cultural heritage today. Every community deserves to represent its own stories and traditions.
